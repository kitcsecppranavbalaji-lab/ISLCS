Intelligent Street Lighting Control System

A smart way to light our roads. The system turns street lights on and off, adjusts their brightness based on traffic and people, and saves energy without compromising safety.

About the Project

What an intelligent street lighting system is and how it works.

What is it?

An Intelligent Street Lighting System uses sensors, a controller and a monitoring dashboard to manage street lights automatically. Instead of burning at full power all night, each light responds to what is happening on the road.

How it works

  1. Sense: sensors detect vehicles and pedestrians.
  2. Decide: the controller chooses the right brightness.
  3. Act: the lamp dims or brightens automatically.
  4. Monitor: energy use is tracked on a dashboard.

Project Objectives

⚡

Save energy

Reduce electricity consumption by lowering light when no one is around.

🛡️

Full brightness whenever vehicles or pedestrians are detected.

📊
